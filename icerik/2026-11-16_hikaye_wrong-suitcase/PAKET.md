Metin (A2–B1): I got home from the airport at midnight. I was exhausted. I opened my suitcase to find my pajamas. But these were not my clothes. / Inside, there was a blue dress, a camera, and an old notebook. On the first page, someone wrote: “If you find this, please don’t call the airline.” / “Call me.” Below, there was a phone number. I was curious. It was late, but I called. A woman answered after one ring. / “Finally,” she said. “I have your suitcase too. And I opened it.” “We need to talk about the letter in your bag.” I didn’t have a letter in my bag.
SON (ertesi gün hikâyede; uygulamaya eklenirse kare 5 "Sonu uygulamada" olur): “It’s my letter,” she said. “I wrote it to my sister ten years ago. I never sent it. At the airport, I put it in the wrong suitcase. Yours.” She was quiet for a moment. “Maybe it’s a sign. Can you send it for me?” The next morning, I did.
